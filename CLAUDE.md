# Ground rules

Set by the repository owner (James Cosma). They apply to this repository and to every other
project in this account. Follow them in every session.

## Do not assume the product

- Never assume how the owner wants the product to look, work, or be scoped.
- When a request is underspecified, ask. Do not pick a plausible default and build on it.
- Do not infer features, behavior, or scope that the owner did not state.
- Batch every open question into one message instead of asking them one at a time.

## Design decisions belong to the owner

- Any design choice that is a matter of preference is the owner's to make: colors, layout,
  typography, spacing, UX patterns, and user-facing copy or naming.
- Proposing options with a recommendation is welcome. Deciding silently is not.

## Work efficiently

- Preserve tokens. Read only what the task needs, do not re-read what is already known, and do
  not explore beyond the question at hand.
- Report results tersely. Do not narrate work before doing it.

## Correctness comes first

- Logic, intent, and accuracy outrank brevity and speed. If getting it right takes more work, do
  the work.
- Match the owner's stated intent exactly, not an improved version of it.
- Verify changes before reporting them done, and state plainly what was and was not verified.
